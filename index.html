About us

Technology that fits the realities of Somalia.

Azahn Solutions delivers practical, reliable, and scalable technology solutions that help government institutions, NGOs, international development organizations, donors, businesses, and other relevant partners improve service delivery, strengthen institutional capacity, digitize operations, and solve real-world challenges through technology.

We work with public institutions, development partners, and private organizations to identify operational challenges and develop technology solutions tailored to Somalia’s context. Our focus is not simply building software, but delivering systems and digital infrastructure that improve efficiency, transparency, accountability, coordination, and decision-making.
